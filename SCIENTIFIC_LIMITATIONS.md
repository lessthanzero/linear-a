# Scientific Limitations (v0.2.0 research preview)

Linear A remains **undeciphered**. This repository is evidence-management and inspection infrastructure, not a translation or recovered-language system.

## Scope

- Tools may report **structural patterns**, accounting constraints, damaged-token censuses, and curated formula hypotheses.
- They do **not** establish grammar, phonology, meaning, or decipherment.
- The Phaistos–Linear A bridge is an **exploratory analogy** layer only. Concordance labels such as `EXPLORATORY` / `STRUCTURAL_ANALOGY` must never be read as **HOMOLOGY CONFIRMED**.

## Data licensing

Raw **SigLA / GORILA**-derived dumps are **not MIT** and are intentionally excluded from the public default tree (see [NOTICE](NOTICE)). Curated YAML citations are working attributions, not a redistribution of GORILA plates or SigLA database dumps. Cite primary editions when publishing results that depend on them.

## What would change the status

Independent primary-edition adjudication of restorations, published lexical pairs meeting the benchmark’s entry rules, or a bilingual / securely linked phonetic bridge — none of which this preview claims to supply.
